- Skill: superpowers:systematic-debugging, phases 1-2 (reproduce, root cause). Done from the code; live server not checked.
- Root cause (from code):
  - The WhatsMine `message.received` webhook is fired by `MessageReceived` (`app/Modules/Whatsapp/Services/WhatsappDriver.php:705`) and sent by `DispatchWebhookJob` on the default queue.
  - The default queue is drained by `drain-queues` every minute (`routes/console.php:79-82`). That is the "every minute" the user expects. It sets the delivery latency, not a poll of WAHA.
  - `whatsapp-web-sync` runs every 10 minutes (`routes/console.php:86-89`). It backfills messages WhatsMine has not stored. A backfilled message fires `MessageReceived` only when it is under 5 minutes old (`WhatsappDriver.php:684-705`).
  - The screenshot pairs (11:31 x2, 11:41 x2) sit one minute after the 10-minute ticks. That matches the sync job running in the next `drain-queues` run. So the two messages in each pair were stored by the sync, not by the live WAHA webhook.
  - Why the live webhook did not store them is unknown. Unverified on the live server.
